# Bell character, notes (maker: Claude Opus 5.5)

Palette: plum coat, near-black satin lapels and cuff, slate waistcoat, brass and braid, ash-blond hair cooling to the tips, green eyes. Saturation kept for brass, eyes and a teal lining.

Extras: window reflection and sparks on the brass, "HOTEL LACUNA" on the cuff, "ROOM 307" on the key, damask on the waistcoat, stepped embroidery and keyholes on the tails, braid ticks, gold piping on the satin, star on the epaulette button, bell rings, drifting room plaques and keyholes.

Checked: regions, masks, greyscale at 96 px, crops of head, torso, tails. Ring drawn whole and cut only at the parting; sclera shade placed from the lash edge (both lessons from the tape run).

Four self-review rounds. Colour marks about 463, corrections 15 force shapes and 8 walls, cast rules 33, placed shapes about 254. Rules untouched.

Weakest: long coat folds are plain for their size; eyes are tiny, so bands barely read; embroidery corners are thin lines; some hair tips lose a pixel of colour; contact shade greys the pale gloves slightly; neck is a large flat shade.
